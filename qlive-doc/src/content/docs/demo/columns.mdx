The view below is `grid/Columns` from qlive-test, unchanged, running in your
browser on data recorded from the running application.

A field path alone is a column; the object form overrides one thing at a time
and derives the rest. Here: a heading of its own, a path through a relation
(`fooType.name`) that sorts and filters like a field of the row, a class for
large numbers, long text cut off at a width, a computed column sorted by the
expression that computes it, and a column that neither sorts nor filters.
