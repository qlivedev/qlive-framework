The view below is `grid/Search` from qlive-test, unchanged, running in your
browser on data recorded from the running application.

The document has one condition with two owners: the form writes its part, the
grid's filter row its own, and the condition is both joined with `and()`. Each
narrows what the other shows, and neither touches the other's part -- clear the
search, and the grid's filters stay.
