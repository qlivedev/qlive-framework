The view below is `grid/Sum` from qlive-test, unchanged, running in your
browser on data recorded from the running application.

The sum column is no field of the table. It renders `numA + numB` and sorts and
filters by the same expression, `field("numA").add(field("numB"))`: a click on
its header toggles it like a field, and `between` gives a range of sums. In the
application the server evaluates the expression in SQL; here the page does.
The form above narrows the rows to a range of days.
